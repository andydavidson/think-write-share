Think–Write–Share

A simple, anonymous way to gather everyone’s honest thoughts — before the loudest voice sets the tone.

Think

The facilitator poses a question and starts a silent timer. Everyone reflects independently — no discussion, no anchoring on others’ views.

Write

When the timer ends, an answer box appears. Everyone submits their own response anonymously — no names, no accounts, nothing stored that could identify you.

Share

The facilitator closes the session. Everyone sees one randomly chosen anonymous response. Discussion begins from a foundation of honest, independent thought.

  • No groupthink — everyone forms their own view before hearing others
  • Quiet voices count as much as loud ones
  • Fully anonymous — no accounts, no tracking, no participant data stored
  • Works for retrospectives, check-ins, decisions, and structured feedback
